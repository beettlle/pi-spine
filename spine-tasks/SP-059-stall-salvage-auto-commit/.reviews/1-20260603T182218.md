### Summary

Step 1 correctly delivers FR-STALL-03B's opt-in surface and refusal contract on top of SP-058 salvage inspection: `lanes.autoCommitOnStall` defaults to **false** in `templates/spine-config.json`, `validateSpineConfig` rejects non-boolean values, and `resolveSalvageConfig` treats only `=== true` as enabled (missing key and explicit `false` behave the same).
