### Non-blocking notes

- **Untracked core files.** `src/batch/worker-output.mjs` and `tests/batch/stall-output.test.mjs` are required for this step but do not appear in `git diff --name-only` (`??` in status). Include both in the Step 3/4 commit before merge.
- **Journal payload cap vs capture max.** `captureWorkerOutputTail` defaults to 262 KiB; `capPayloadSize` limits any journal event to 32 KiB. Very large captured tails can collapse the entire `task.failed` payload into `_truncated` / `preview`, weakening structured `output` in the journal even when the on-disk log is correct. Consider aligning journal-bound output size with `MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` or field-wise capping in a follow-up.
- **`task.failed` diagnosis ordering.** `extractJournalDiagnosisHints` lists `task.failed` before `lane.stall_killed`; the first hint may show only `classification` while the log path appears on the second hint. Acceptable for stall repro; optional polish: surface `workerOutputLogRef` in `summarizeJournalEvent` for `task.failed`.
- **Absolute path in journal.** Spreading full `workerResult` also writes `workerOutputLogPath` (absolute filesystem path) into `task.failed`. Prefer omitting absolute paths from journal payloads if tightening later.
- **Step 3 vs Step 4 boundary.** Stub hang hooks (`bin/spine-worker-runner.mjs`) and broad `stall-output.test.mjs` overlap Step 4 in PROMPT; acceptable in one branch, but split commits if you want strict step hygiene.
